Halter helps users reduce compulsive phone use with local app limits, short-video scroll counting, breathing pauses, deep focus sessions, and honest reports.

The app is designed for privacy and F-Droid compatibility. It does not request internet access, does not include Firebase or analytics SDKs, and keeps all data on the device.

Core features:

* Accessibility-based foreground app detection
* Usage-session tracking for daily and session limits
* Scroll event logging for short-video surfaces
* Breathing pause and session-limit picker before flagged apps
* Full-screen block overlays
* Deep Focus sessions
* Local reports, journal, widget, and quick settings tile

Halter is GPL-3.0-or-later free software.
